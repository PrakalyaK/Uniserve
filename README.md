# 🤝 UniServ

### Cooperative-Powered Gig Services Platform for Households & Communities

**UniServ** is a cooperative-powered digital platform that connects households and communities with **verified skilled workers** while enabling cooperatives to manage their workforce, services, bookings, and worker welfare.

Built for **Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Problem Statement 26089**.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Many skilled workers such as **electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, cleaners, caregivers, drivers, gardeners, and technicians** operate through informal or fragmented service networks.

This creates challenges in:

* **Finding verified workers**
* **Fair distribution of work**
* **Managing worker availability**
* **Tracking service requests**
* **Ensuring payment transparency**
* **Providing worker welfare**
* **Handling emergency service requests**

**UniServ** addresses these challenges by creating a centralized **cooperative-owned service ecosystem**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

UniServ brings **customers, workers, and cooperative administrators** together on a single platform.

### 👤 Customers

* **Discover services** based on their requirements.
* **Find verified workers** with relevant skills.
* **Book services** for scheduled or emergency requirements.
* **Track service status**.
* **Make payments** through a structured payment workflow.
* **Rate and review workers** after service completion.

### 🧑‍🔧 Workers

* **Create and manage professional profiles**.
* **Add skills and certifications**.
* **Manage availability**.
* **Receive suitable job assignments**.
* **Track workload and earnings**.
* **Access worker welfare and protection features**.

### 🏢 Cooperative Administrators

* **Verify workers and certifications**.
* **Manage workers and service categories**.
* **Monitor bookings and service requests**.
* **Manage workforce availability**.
* **Track cooperative operations**.
* **View analytics and demand trends**.

---

# ⭐ Key Features

## 🔐 Worker Verification

Cooperatives can verify workers based on their:

* **Skills**
* **Certifications**
* **Experience**
* **Service categories**

This helps customers discover **trusted and verified service providers**.

---

## ⚖️ Fair Work Allocation

UniServ includes a **Fairness Engine** designed to distribute work using multiple factors instead of relying only on proximity.

The allocation considers:

* **Skill compatibility**
* **Worker availability**
* **Current workload**
* **Location**
* **Worker rating**
* **Service requirements**

```text
Skill Compatibility
        +
Availability
        +
Current Workload
        +
Location
        +
Rating
        ↓
Fair Worker Matching
```

---

## 📍 Location-Aware Matching

Customers can discover suitable workers based on:

* **Required service**
* **Worker skills**
* **Availability**
* **Geographical proximity**

This helps reduce unnecessary travel while improving service response time.

---

## 📅 Smart Booking

UniServ supports different types of service requests:

* **Scheduled bookings**
* **On-demand services**
* **Emergency requests**

Customers can track the booking throughout its lifecycle.

---

## 🔑 Dual OTP Verification

UniServ uses separate OTP verification points to establish a clear service lifecycle.

```text
Customer Booking
       ↓
   Worker Assigned
       ↓
     Start OTP
       ↓
 Service Begins
       ↓
  Service Completed
       ↓
  Completion OTP
       ↓
 Payment Settlement
```

This helps improve **service verification and dispute handling**.

---

## 💳 Transparent Payment Workflow

The platform supports a structured payment flow where payment settlement is linked to the **completion of the service**.

This creates greater transparency between:

**Customer → Worker → Cooperative**

---

## 🚨 Emergency Services

Customers can create **urgent service requests** for situations requiring immediate assistance.

The platform can prioritize suitable **nearby and available workers** for emergency requests.

---

## 🛡️ Worker Welfare

UniServ goes beyond simply connecting customers and workers.

The platform provides a dedicated space for **worker welfare and protection**, helping cooperatives support their members beyond individual service bookings.

---

## 📊 Cooperative Dashboard

Cooperative administrators can monitor:

* **Active workers**
* **Bookings**
* **Service categories**
* **Worker availability**
* **Earnings**
* **Demand**
* **Operational activity**

This provides cooperatives with a centralized view of their service ecosystem.
